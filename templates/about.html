About Flight Delay Prediction System

Project Introduction

The Flight Delay Prediction System is a machine learning-based web application designed to predict whether a flight is likely to be delayed based on historical flight records and operational factors. The system analyzes various flight attributes such as airline, departure and arrival airports, departure time, flight distance, weather-related delays, and previous delay information to provide accurate delay predictions.

Objectives

  • Analyze historical flight data to identify delay patterns.
  • Predict whether a flight will be delayed or on time.
  • Identify the major factors contributing to flight delays.
  • Assist passengers in better travel planning.
  • Support airlines in improving operational efficiency and scheduling.

Dataset Features

  • Airline
  • Origin Airport
  • Destination Airport
  • Departure Delay
  • Arrival Delay
  • Air Time
  • Distance
  • Taxi In / Taxi Out Time
  • Weather Delay
  • Security Delay
  • Late Aircraft Delay
  • Delay Status (Target Variable)
